# Pentagon pairs for specific cardboard spools

Matched gear-side and cap-side centering rings for spool holes that fall between the 1 mm [stock sizes](../stock-sizes/README.md). They use the same pentagon design and the same generator; a fractional size keeps the holder bore and snap ribs of the next smaller stock ring.

| Nominal hole | Gear side | Cap side |
|---|---|---|
| 53.4 mm (Elegoo Rapid PETG cardboard spool) | [3MF](models/kraken-spool-ring-53.4-mm-pentagon-gear-side-1.5mm-flange.3mf) · [STL](models/kraken-spool-ring-53.4-mm-pentagon-gear-side-1.5mm-flange.stl) | [3MF](models/kraken-spool-ring-53.4-mm-pentagon-cap-side.3mf) · [STL](models/kraken-spool-ring-53.4-mm-pentagon-cap-side.stl) |

The 55.5 mm Polymaker pair is on the [rings page](../README.md). Geometry checks for this pair are in [VALIDATION.json](VALIDATION.json); physical fit is reported only where the table on the rings page says it was measured.
