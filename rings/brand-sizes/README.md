# Pentagon pairs for specific cardboard spools

Matched gear-side and cap-side centering rings for spool holes that fall between the 1 mm [stock sizes](../stock-sizes/README.md). They use the same pentagon design and the same generator; a fractional size keeps the holder bore and snap ribs of the next smaller stock ring. The five corners bite into cardboard; for hard plastic hubs use the [spring pairs](../hard-spool/README.md).

**Flange sets.** *Normal*: 2.2 mm gear-side flange and 0.6 mm cap-side lip. *Skinny*: 0.4 mm on both sides, for holders where the extra 2.0 mm of stack does not fit. humebeam's original 0.4 mm cap lip tends to break during bed removal and handling, which is why normal thickens it. The spigot of each side rises by the added base thickness, so it still inserts as deep as the stock ring.
