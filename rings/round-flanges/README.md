# Round ring pairs

The earlier round centering rings: humebeam's round outer profile, with the 0.4 mm lip thickened per flange set. The gear-side flange reaches **20 mm radially beyond the hole**, wider than the later [pentagon family](../stock-sizes/README.md) with its 10.25 mm reach. The cap side keeps the lip's 2.5 mm reach. For cardboard spools the pentagon pairs grip better; for hard plastic spools use the [spring pairs](../hard-spool/README.md).

**Flange sets.** *Normal*: 2.2 mm gear-side flange and 0.6 mm cap-side lip. *Skinny*: 0.4 mm on both sides (the skinny cap side is humebeam's ring unchanged). humebeam's 0.4 mm cap lip tends to break during bed removal and handling, which is why normal thickens it.

Choose by measured spool hole. The family has not been physically fit-tested across every size. STL coordinates retain the original source placement: centre the part and move its flat flange underside onto the bed in your slicer. The model files do not contain print settings.

## Reproduction

[Validation](VALIDATION.json) checks all 204 meshes as single closed solids. From the repository root, using its CAD dependencies:

```sh
python rings/source/round_flange.py rings/source/reference round-build normal
python rings/source/round_flange.py rings/source/reference round-skinny-build skinny
```

The script remaps humebeam's mesh in place: heights 0–0.4 mm stretch to the new base thickness, everything above moves up by the difference, and gear-side lip vertices move outward to the 20 mm reach. It does not scale the holder bore radially. At 1.5 mm the same remap reproduces the earlier published gear-side files byte for byte apart from the STL header. Original meshes are attributed in the [source notes](../source/README.md). The changed flange does not by itself prove increased gear-to-body clearance.
